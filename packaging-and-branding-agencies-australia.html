What to know

Before you brief.

A branding and packaging agency does more than design a pack. It sets the strategy and brand identity first, then designs the packaging to match, so everything from the logo to the label tells one story. That end to end control matters most when you are launching or repositioning across a whole range.

The agencies below all do both branding and packaging in Australia. Shortlist two or three whose work fits your category and budget, and brief them the same way. For an independent read on your current pack first, start with the Packaging Performance Review.

Questions

Common questions.

What is the difference between a packaging designer and a branding agency?

A packaging designer or studio focuses on the pack itself. A branding and packaging agency also handles strategy, naming and brand identity, then carries that through to the packaging so the whole system is consistent. Match the partner to how much you need built.

Do I need a full branding and packaging agency?

If you only need one pack refreshed, a studio or freelancer is usually enough. If you are launching or repositioning a brand across a range, an agency that does both branding and packaging keeps strategy, identity and pack aligned.

How much does a branding and packaging agency cost in Australia?

A single pack can start in the low thousands. A full brand and range program from an agency runs into the tens of thousands, depending on scope. See the cost guide for current Australian ranges.
